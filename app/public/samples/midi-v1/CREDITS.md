# zvid MIDI sample: credits

These files are the media for the zvid MIDI sample (File → Open → Samples →
MIDI). Keep this file with them when you redistribute the sample or an
export of it.

## Music

There is no recorded audio. Every sound is generated in zvid from the
session's MIDI clips: the Drum Rack plays its TR-808 bank (synthesized by
`app/scripts/gen-drum-banks.mjs`), the Synths and Arpeggiator generate the
bass, arp and lead, and the Granular plays grains of its built-in tone.

## Animation

`forest-morning.lottie` ("Forest Morning") and `gradient-dots.lottie`
("Gradient Dots Background") are dotLottie animations from LottieFiles
(https://lottiefiles.com). The author in their manifests is LottieFiles.
They are free animations under the Lottie Simple License
(https://lottiefiles.com/page/license) and were supplied for this sample in
issue #1415.

`background-full-screen.lottie` ("Background Full Screen") and
`background-full-screen-train.lottie` ("Background Full Screen", its train
variant) are dotLottie animations from LottieFiles (https://lottiefiles.com).
The author in their manifests is LottieFiles. They are free animations under
the Lottie Simple License (https://lottiefiles.com/page/license) and were
supplied for this sample in issue #1435.

## Logo

`zvid-logo.svg` is the zvid logo, zvid's own artwork.
