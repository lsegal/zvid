# zvid opening sample: credits

These files are the media for the zvid opening sample (File → Open Sample).
Keep this file with them when you redistribute the sample or an export of it.

`decisions-30s.m4a` is an excerpt of the first 30 seconds with a 0.6-second
fade-in and a two-second fade-out, synchronized to the sample's graphics.

## Video

`orbit.mp4`, `ribbon.mp4` and `corridor.mp4` are original procedural motion
clips made for zvid (1920×1080, 30 fps, 16 seconds each) by
`app/scripts/sample/render_sources.py`. They are clean sources: every cut,
arrangement, effect and title in the sample is built in zvid itself.
