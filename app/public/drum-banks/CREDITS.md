# zvid built-in drum banks: credits

These are the drum banks listed in the Drum Rack effect's bank menu. Keep
this file with them when you redistribute them.

Every sound here is synthesized by zvid: `app/scripts/gen-drum-banks.mjs`
models each one on how its drum machine makes it and renders it as a
44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono `.wav` file. None is sampled from a
machine or taken from a third-party sample pack, so none carries a
third-party license; they are distributed under zvid's own license. Bank
names describe the machine a bank imitates and are trademarks of their
owners, who do not endorse zvid.
