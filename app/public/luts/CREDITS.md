# zvid built-in LUTs: credits

These are the film-look LUTs listed under "Built-in" in the LUT effect's
picker. Keep this file with them when you redistribute them.

Every LUT here is generated by zvid: `app/scripts/gen-film-luts.mjs`
samples a documented color transform for each look on a 17×17×17
grid. None is taken from a third-party pack, so none carries a third-party
license; they are distributed under zvid's own license. Look names describe
the look and are not film-stock trademarks.
